Capstone Project
Monthly Expense and Budget Analysis

PROJECT BASED LEARNING FROM JAVA

Abstract

This software helps people keep track of their income, expenses and savings in a clear and easy way. It records expenses, compares them with a planned budget, shows where spending is higher than expected, and saves data so users can look back at previous months.
